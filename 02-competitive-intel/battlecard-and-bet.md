## 1. Primary competitor

- **Competitor:** Airbnb Experiences
- **Core offering:** They offer a platform for local organizers to advertise their experiences, adventures etc. as well as booking, reservation sharing, and payment processing
- **How they position themselves:** Discover and book memorable experiences led by local hosts, including experiences that can be reserved privately for a group.
- **Primary audience:** It's a global audience but travelers everywhere.
- **Known pricing or packaging:** Local experts charge their own price. Airbnb takes a cut.
- **Why they're the biggest threat:** They are a pseudo monopoly and largest platform for selling local rentals and experiences. They already have a global reach and user ship. Splitting reservations and collecting from others and selling culinary experiences would just be a new feature or feature set.

#### 1. KNOW THE LANDSCAPE

##### Competitor Overview
Airbnb Experiences is an established global marketplace connecting travelers with locally hosted activities. It benefits from Airbnb's brand recognition, traveler audience, extensive host network, and booking infrastructure.

##### Key Offerings
- Curated culinary, cultural, and recreational experiences.
- Private group bookings and customizable experiences.
- Guest invitations, messaging, and shared reservation details.
- Host profiles, reviews, and integrated payments.

##### Pricing
- Hosts determine per-person pricing and private-group minimums.
- Airbnb generally charges hosts a 20% service fee.
- Guests see the total experience price without an additional guest service fee.
- A single reservation cannot currently be split across multiple guests' credit cards.
